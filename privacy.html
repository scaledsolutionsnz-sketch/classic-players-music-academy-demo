Privacy Policy

How Classic Players Music Academy handles the information you share with us.

This is placeholder content prepared for the demo website and should be reviewed before publishing.

1. Introduction

Classic Players Music Academy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights, in line with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us when enquiring about or attending lessons, such as:

  • Student and parent/guardian names
  • Contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Details relevant to lessons, such as instrument, level and preferred times

Because this website has no contact form, any information you send reaches us directly by phone or email.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, arrange and deliver lessons, communicate about scheduling, and keep you informed about the academy. We do not sell your information.

4. Sharing your information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to deliver our services or where required by law.

5. Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to limit access to those who need it to provide lessons and respond to enquiries.

6. Third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as Google Maps to display our location and Google Fonts for typography. These providers may process limited technical data (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Call 027 291 8236 or email us via the contact section on our homepage.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this website.
