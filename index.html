About the academy

A classical foundation, taught with patience

Classic Players Music Academy began in 2008 under founder Anil Cheriyan as a place to learn music properly — vocal, instrumental and theory, rooted in both Carnatic and Western classical traditions. That same approach now guides students here in Dunedin.

Lessons are one-to-one and unhurried. Beginners build good habits from day one; more advanced players push toward graded exams, recitals and repertoire they're proud to perform. Every student is met where they are.

  • One-to-one lessons tailored to each student's pace and goals
  • Classically grounded — Carnatic and Western training
  • Clear pathway to Trinity & ABRSM grades and recitals
